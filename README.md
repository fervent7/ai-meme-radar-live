
# AI Meme Radar

## AI-powered intelligence for emerging meme-token markets

AI Meme Radar is an actively developed intelligence system designed to discover, analyze and filter emerging meme-token activity across multiple blockchain networks.

The goal is not simply to produce more token alerts.

The goal is to help distinguish meaningful market activity from noise by combining market data, safety analysis, holder and buyer verification, growth analysis and conservative signal qualification.

---

## What we're building

AI Meme Radar is being developed around several core intelligence layers:

- 🔎 Multi-source token discovery
- 📊 Market activity analysis
- 🛡️ Safety and risk analysis
- 👥 Holder and buyer verification
- 📈 Growth detection
- 🧠 AI-assisted intelligence
- 📡 Conservative signal qualification
- 📱 Community intelligence
- 📚 Historical performance tracking

The system is being developed progressively, with reliability and data quality taking priority over alert volume.

---

## Development approach

AI Meme Radar is being built in public.

Our development loop is:

**BUILD → TEST → MEASURE → IMPROVE → DOCUMENT → REPEAT**

The public repository documents the direction, progress, methodology and milestones of the project.

The production implementation remains private while the system continues to evolve.

## Current status

**Status: Active Development**

The Radar is already running as an experimental multi-source intelligence system.

Current development areas include:

- Multi-chain discovery
- Market intelligence
- Safety screening
- Holder verification
- Buyer verification
- Growth tracking
- Signal qualification
- Provider reliability
- Persistent historical data
- Telegram integration

The system is intentionally conservative.

A lack of alerts does not necessarily mean a lack of opportunities. It can also mean that available data does not yet provide enough evidence to qualify a signal.
