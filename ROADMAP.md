# AI Meme Radar — Roadmap

AI Meme Radar is being developed as a product-first intelligence ecosystem.

The roadmap is intentionally progressive: reliability and useful data come before expansion.

---

## Phase 1 — Radar Foundation

**Status: In progress**

Build a reliable foundation for discovering and evaluating emerging meme-token activity.

- Multi-source token discovery
- Multi-chain discovery
- Market activity analysis
- Safety and risk screening
- Holder verification
- Buyer verification
- Growth detection
- Conservative signal qualification
- Telegram integration
- Diagnostics and monitoring

---

## Phase 2 — Intelligence Layer

**Next development stage**

Turn raw market activity into more useful intelligence.

- Improved token intelligence profiles
- Historical activity analysis
- Stronger growth detection
- Risk intelligence
- Market-context analysis
- Signal explanations
- Radar performance measurement

---

## Phase 3 — Community Intelligence

Build a community around the information produced by the Radar.

- Telegram community
- Community feedback
- Signal discussion
- Research sharing
- Educational content
- Community reputation
- Contribution tracking

The objective is to combine automated intelligence with human observation.

---

## Phase 4 — Wallet & Market Intelligence

Expand beyond individual token discovery.

- Wallet intelligence
- Wallet activity analysis
- Behaviour patterns
- Related-wallet research
- Market movement analysis
- Advanced risk signals

These capabilities will be introduced gradually and only when the underlying data is reliable enough to support them.

---

## Phase 5 — Radar Track Record

Measure the system instead of simply claiming that it works.

- Historical signals
- Signal outcomes
- Performance measurement
- False-positive analysis
- Missed-opportunity analysis
- Methodology improvements
- Public performance reporting

This phase is important for establishing credibility.

---

## Phase 6 — Ecosystem

Expand AI Meme Radar beyond a single detection system.

Potential areas include:

- Research tools
- Education / Academy
- Developer tools
- API access
- Integrations
- Community contribution systems
- Reputation systems
- Advanced analytics

---

## Phase 7 — Future Economic Layer

Only after the product, data, community and ecosystem have demonstrated genuine utility.

Potential areas may include:

- Contribution rewards
- Ecosystem incentives
- Governance
- Access mechanisms
- Community coordination
- Future token utility

There is currently **no AI Meme Radar token**.

A token is not the starting point of the project.

The intended sequence is:

**Product → Data → Community → Reputation → Utility → Ecosystem → Token**

---

## Guiding Principle

AI Meme Radar will prioritize:

**Reliability over hype.**

**Evidence over assumptions.**

**Useful intelligence over alert volume.**

**Product development before token creation.**
