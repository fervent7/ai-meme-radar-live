# AI Meme Radar — Ecosystem Architecture

## Overview

AI Meme Radar is being developed as an intelligence platform for emerging meme-token markets.

The Radar is not intended to remain a simple token-alert system.

The long-term objective is to build an ecosystem where market discovery, intelligence, research, monitoring, community participation and future developer tools are connected through a common intelligence layer.

The underlying principle is:

**One intelligence engine → multiple useful products and services.**

---

## The Core Intelligence Engine

The foundation of the ecosystem is the AI Meme Radar intelligence layer.

It brings together available information from multiple sources and evaluates it across several dimensions.

The core intelligence areas include:

- Token discovery
- Market activity
- Liquidity
- Buy and sell activity
- Holder activity
- Buyer activity
- Growth
- Safety indicators
- Risk indicators
- Wallet activity
- Historical observations
- Social and community information

The objective is to transform fragmented market information into useful intelligence.
